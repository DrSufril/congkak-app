Congkak: Support

Congkak is the traditional Malay seed game for iPhone and iPad. Play against the computer or with a friend on the same device.

Contact

For questions, problems or ideas, email sufril@usm.my. You can also tap About › Send feedback in the app.

Common questions

How do I learn the rules?
Tap How to play on the start screen, then try the interactive tutorial.

How do two people play on one device?
Tap 2 Players. Player 1 plays the bottom row and Player 2 the top row, so you can sit across from each other.

Does my progress move to my other devices?
Yes, if iCloud is on and you use the same Apple Account on both devices.

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads and no purchases.

Privacy

Congkak collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
