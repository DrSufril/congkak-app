Congkak: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Congkak is a game made by Associate Professor Dr. Ahmad Sufril Azlan Mohamed. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

We collect no personal data

Congkak does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your game progress

Your level, badges, settings and other game progress are saved on your device. If iCloud is turned on, the same progress is also kept in your own iCloud account so it can appear on your other Apple devices. The developer cannot see or access this data. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device only. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Congkak is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal data, no information about children is gathered.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback by email, your message and email address are used only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
